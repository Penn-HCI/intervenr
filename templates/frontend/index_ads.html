Want to help researchers better understand online advertising? You can join us in three easy steps.

1
Sign up for the waitlist
First, you'll sign up to join the waitlist by completing a brief background survey so that we can select a diverse pool of participants. Please note that you must be at least 18 years or older, have a Google account, and use Google Chrome as your main browser in order to participate.
2
Onboard to the study
Second, if you are selected to participate in the study, we will send you instructions to onboard you to the study. In this phase, you will install the Intervenr Chrome extension; you must have access to Chrome you must install the Intervenr Extension. In order to install the Intervenr Extension, you must again have access to (and primarily use) Google Chrome as your web browser. After you have finished installing and registering the extension, you must continue to use the Chrome browser for the duration of the study.
3
Participate in the study
After creating an account and activating the Intervenr Chrome extension, you will then be fully enrolled in the study. During the study period, you will be asked to complete a midpoint and final survey. Upon completion of the study period and both surveys, you will receive your study compensation.
